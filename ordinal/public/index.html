About This Site

I started journaling in 2018 for various reasons, project logs, technical references, life experiences, therapy notes. A lot of ideas I did not want to lose. Over time, this grew into a large private knowledge base with over a thousand entries, loosely connected, written as I went.

Most of this material was never meant to be public. This is not a blog or a collection of polished essays. Much of what’s here consists of working notes. You will see questions, false starts, partial ideas, and changes in direction. Sometimes the answers are unfinished, but the path to reach them is there.

This site is not written for a specific audience. It exists first for myself, but I hope that you can find something useful for you.

Ordinal is the system I built to present this material. It preserves the structure of my notes and the relationships between them.

This is a record of sustained effort. Taken together, these entries are the clearest account I can provide of my work and the experiences that have shaped me.
